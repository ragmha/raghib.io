# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is a broad mix of curious technical and non-technical
readers across industries: builders, engineering leaders, operators, and
decision-makers interested in how software and AI reshape systems,
organizations, and work.

Readers should encounter a clear point of view without needing knowledge of
Raghib Hasan's employer, job title, or professional context.

## Product Purpose

raghib.io is Raghib Hasan's independent publishing home. It develops practical,
cross-industry ideas about software, AI, systems, governance, and technology
adoption, connecting essays to experiments, projects, and source code where
useful.

Success means a visitor can quickly understand the site's intellectual focus,
find a relevant article, read it comfortably, and follow an idea into evidence,
an experiment, or a project.

## Positioning

The site presents technology as a set of choices with consequences. Posts begin
with a meaningful question, examine the surrounding system, make trade-offs
visible, and leave readers with an idea they can apply in their own context.

## Operating Context

Visitors typically arrive from a shared article, a search result, a GitHub
project, or a professional profile. They browse the home page, writing archive,
individual long-form posts, and curated projects.

Posts are authored as MDX and may include arguments, architecture explanations,
implementation steps, code, diagrams, observations, security considerations,
and links to working repositories.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Astro, TypeScript, plain CSS, and Bun are the implementation stack.
- Existing MDX publishing, RSS, post metadata, syntax highlighting, responsive behavior, and GitHub project integration must continue to work.
- The current terminal simulation is not a product requirement and may be removed.
- The site should be fast, accessible, easy to maintain, and comfortable for long-form reading.
- The public site should be statically generated and require no application runtime or client-side framework.
- Real project and article content must be preserved; the redesign must not invent customers, outcomes, or metrics.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Raghib Hasan.
- Identity: an independent technology writer and practitioner, not a
  spokesperson for an employer or product.
- Voice: curious, direct, technically credible, practical, and understandable
  outside a narrow specialist audience.
- The layout should follow the restraint and direct navigation of matchaonmuffins.dev rather than a conventional animated developer portfolio.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing MDX articles in `src/content/writing/`.
- Existing GitHub-backed project archive.
- Existing GitHub and LinkedIn profile links.
- The article model combines a clear thesis with practical evidence,
  implementation detail, trade-offs, and ideas that travel across industries.
- No testimonials, customer logos, performance benchmarks, or employer-approved case-study claims are available and none should be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. Establish a clear point of view before displaying credentials.
2. Lead with consequential questions and original thinking, not visual spectacle.
3. Keep policies, architecture, code, and evidence visible enough to earn trust.
4. Make every page useful beyond a single company, product, or industry.
5. Prefer durable publishing conventions over portfolio gimmicks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site should meet WCAG 2.2 AA expectations, support keyboard navigation and reduced motion, preserve clear focus states, and maintain readable line lengths and contrast across mobile and desktop.
